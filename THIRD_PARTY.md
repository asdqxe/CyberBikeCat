- `crossterm` 0.27.0 is the terminal dependency declared in Cargo.toml.
  Cargo.lock records the resolved dependency graph. Dependencies retain their
  own licenses; the project's MIT license does not replace them.
- No code, fonts or pet sprite sheets from the research reference repositories
  were imported during the current implementation. The pet glyphs are locally
  authored with AI assistance; see AI_USE.md.
- The green NVIDIA text and simplified eye-shaped sign are a hand-drawn ASCII
  interpretation, not an official image asset. This project is not affiliated
  with or endorsed by NVIDIA. The project license does not grant trademark rights.
- The original teaching example and uncompiled wgpu sketch were supplied by the
  project owner from Claude output; see AI_USE.md for provenance.

This is a source-preview inventory, not a completed audit of all transitive
dependency notices. Before publishing downloadable binary bundles, review the
licenses and include the notices required by the dependencies actually shipped.
